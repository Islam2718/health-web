# Ibnocare Typography Guidelines

Font choices are context-specific — the web app, printed/exported clinical
documents, and physical pharmacy packaging each use different typefaces
optimized for their medium.

## Clinical Documents & Patient Forms
**Fonts:** Arial, Calibri, Georgia
Used for: printable/exportable prescriptions, lab reports, discharge
summaries, consent forms. These are chosen for maximum legibility at small
sizes, wide compatibility with PDF/print pipelines, and familiarity in
official/medical paperwork. Georgia for headings/section titles on longer
reports, Arial/Calibri for body and tabular data.

## Pharmaceuticals & Packaging
**Font:** Verdana
Used for: medicine labels, dosage instructions, pharmacy receipts, and any
physical/print packaging content generated by the Medical Store module.
Verdana's wide letterforms and generous spacing hold up well at very small
print sizes and under low-quality printing.

## Digital Health Apps & Websites
**Fonts:** Proxima Nova, Frutiger, Helvetica Neue
Used for: the Ibnocare web app and marketing site UI. These are commercial
licensed fonts; since the product ships with free, self-hosted Google Fonts
via `next/font`, we use **Inter** as the production substitute — a
neo-grotesque sans with near-identical metrics/feel to Helvetica
Neue/Frutiger, and the de-facto standard for modern health-tech UI.

If a Proxima Nova/Frutiger license is acquired later, swap the `next/font`
import in `src/app/layout.tsx` — the rest of the system references the
`--font-sans` CSS variable, so no other files need to change.
